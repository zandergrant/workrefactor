Working together

A practical way to make work easier.

Start small, work with the systems you have, and agree on a clear outcome before building anything.

01

Start with a focused review

Systems and workflow review

For a recurring task, messy handoff or process that seems to take more effort than it should. We follow real examples, understand the people and systems involved, and identify where work gets duplicated, delayed or lost.

What you get: A clear summary of the current process, the main sources of friction, practical improvement options and a recommended first change to test. No obligation to commission a build.

Best fit: You know something is inefficient, but you aren't sure what should change first.

02

Fix one workflow

Focused improvement project

For a problem that is understood and ready for a practical solution. The work might mean simplifying steps, clarifying ownership, connecting existing systems, automating reliable tasks or building a small internal web app.

What you get: A scoped improvement, testing with the people who use it, and a clear handoff covering how it works and what to maintain.

Best fit: You want a specific process to run better without committing to a company-wide transformation.

03

Keep improving as you grow

Ongoing systems support

For teams with several connected processes or an internal tool that needs attention as the business changes. We prioritize small, useful improvements and address issues that show up in real use.

What you get: An agreed improvement backlog, periodic review of what is working, and clearly scoped changes as needed.

Best fit: You need continuity without creating another full-time internal role.

How an engagement starts

Send a short description of the problem. We'll respond by email and ask only the questions needed to understand it. If it looks like a fit, we'll propose a scope, deliverables and price before any paid work begins. We don't publish fixed prices because the work varies by systems, access and complexity. A meeting is only necessary when it helps move the work forward.

You don't need a project brief.

One real example of what isn't working is enough to begin.
