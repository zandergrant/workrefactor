Quick answer

What does AI for operations mean?

AI for operations means using AI to perform a defined job within recurring business work. That job might be categorizing an incoming request, extracting fields from a document, preparing a draft, or helping an employee find an approved answer.

A useful implementation specifies the input, expected output, checks, review requirements, and next action. The surrounding workflow controls what happens when the output is missing, wrong, or unsuitable.

Begin with an operating problem: a slow queue, repetitive document handling, difficult knowledge access, or expensive first drafts. Establish the current performance and test whether AI improves the completed work after review and recovery are included.

This guide focuses on language and document tasks in service and knowledge-work businesses. The examples are illustrative workflow designs, not claims about completed client projects.
