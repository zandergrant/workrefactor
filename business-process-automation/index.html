Quick answer

What is business process automation?

Business process automation, often shortened to BPA, uses technology to redesign and automate recurring end-to-end business processes across multiple steps, systems, teams, decisions, and handoffs.

The important word is end-to-end. BPA looks past speeding up a single task. It follows the work from the first trigger to the finished outcome, then asks how to simplify that whole path, make it consistent, connect the systems, and automate what’s left.

A client onboarding process, for example, may begin when a contract is signed and end when the client is fully provisioned, billed, introduced to the delivery team, and ready to begin work. That process may cross sales, finance, operations, project management, document systems, and client communication.
