Selected work · Internal operations hub

One reliable view of launch readiness

The information was already there. The challenge was bringing it together in a way people could trust, without adding another system to maintain.

Tell us what you're working onSee selected work

Context: Previous in-house operating work, shared in general terms. These are not How Work Runs client projects.

01

The situation

Teams responsible for complex, concurrent launches were working from different spreadsheets, reporting systems, training records and conversations. Staffing, training progress, deadlines and capacity could each be checked, but answering a simple question—“Are we ready?”—still meant gathering updates and reconciling them manually.

02

What I built

I designed and built a secure internal web application using the tools already available to the organization. It combined data from multiple teams, reporting and learning systems into a shared operational view. Automated pulls and validation checks helped surface missing or inconsistent information. Teams could continue updating through familiar workflows, rather than maintaining a second set of records.

03

What changed

Instead of requesting another round of status updates, stakeholders could review staffing, training, milestones, capacity and risks together. The shared view made it easier to spot gaps early and coordinate decisions across multiple launch waves.

04

Why this approach

The problem was not a shortage of software. It was the work required to assemble and trust information scattered across existing systems. Building on those systems kept implementation focused and avoided introducing a separate subscription simply to display data already being collected.

Illustrative details have been generalized to protect internal operations. No client-specific records or interfaces are shown.
