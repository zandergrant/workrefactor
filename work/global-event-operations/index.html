Making complex delivery schedules easier to manage

Coordinating people, assignments and changing plans requires more than a calendar.

Tell us what you're working onSee selected work

Context: Previous in-house operating work, shared in general terms. These are not How Work Runs client projects.

01

The situation

A distributed service operation needed consistent visibility into delivery schedules and responsibilities. Changes created repeated coordination across teams and tools.

02

My contribution

I led operational coordination and built practical workflow tools to make schedules and assignments easier to review. The work emphasized clear ownership, dependable updates and checks that reduced manual reconciliation.

03

What changed

The team could work from a more consistent view of planned activity and respond to changes with less duplicated effort.

04

Why this approach

The aim was operational reliability, not calendar automation for its own sake. Tools supported the decisions and judgment of the people responsible for delivery.
