The details

Privacy policy.

We keep the information we ask for limited to what helps us understand your inquiry. Here’s what the website collects, which services are involved, and how to ask about your data.

Last updated November 7, 2026

The short version

The tools and downloads on this site are free to use without an account. If you send a project inquiry, we use the details you provide to review it and respond. We have not added advertising trackers or marketing analytics to this site.

Who this policy covers

This policy applies to howworkruns (How Work Runs) and the website at howworkruns.com. It explains how we handle information from visitors and people who contact us about operations, systems, and automation work.

Information you share

If you complete the project inquiry form, we ask for your name, work email, and a description of the problem you want to solve. You can also choose to provide your company, role, website, business context, budget, timeline, or other details.

We also receive information you send through subsequent correspondence. Please avoid including passwords, payment card details, sensitive personal information, or confidential client records in an initial inquiry.

How we use it

We use inquiry details to understand your request, decide whether we can help, reply to you, and manage any resulting business correspondence. We may also keep records as needed for legitimate business administration or legal obligations.

Submitting an inquiry does not sign you up for a mailing list. We do not sell or rent the personal information you send us.

Services that support the website

  • Contact form: The project inquiry form is designed to use FormSubmit, which processes submitted information and delivers it by email after the receiving address is configured and verified. FormSubmit handles data under its own privacy policy.
  • Hosting: The website is published using GitHub Pages. GitHub may receive ordinary connection data, such as IP addresses and request details, to deliver and secure the site.
  • Fonts: Some text uses fonts loaded from Google Fonts. A browser may connect to Google to retrieve those font files, which can involve standard connection information.

We may also disclose information where required by law or to protect our rights or the security of the service.

Free tools, cookies, and tracking

The interactive audits, demonstrations, and decision tools on this site run in your browser. Using those tools does not send the answers you enter to a howworkruns database. Downloaded templates are files you can save and edit independently.

As of the date above, we have not installed advertising pixels, marketing cookies, or a visitor-analytics service in the website code. The hosting provider and externally loaded services may still process technical connection information as described above. We will update this policy if our use of tracking technology changes.

How long we keep information

We keep inquiry correspondence for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, evaluate a possible project, maintain relevant business records, or meet legal requirements. Service providers may apply their own retention periods.

We take reasonable care when handling inquiries, but no online service or method of email delivery can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices and privacy requests

You can choose not to submit the inquiry form and can use the public resources without providing contact details. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information.

To ask about information you have shared, use the Contact page or reply to an existing email conversation with us. We will review requests in accordance with applicable law.

Changes to this policy

We may revise this page as the website or the services supporting it change. The date above will reflect the latest revision.

This policy describes the website as it is currently configured. It is not a statement that every external provider operates under the same practices.
